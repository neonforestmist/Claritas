# This is made for the OpenAI Discord Challenge

## Claritas — AI Literacy for Everyone

I built Claritas for the OpenAI Discord Challenge at the **Build For Good** event.

Claritas is an interactive AI-literacy app for people who do not have a technical background, people who are still getting comfortable with AI, and people who feel uncertain or afraid because of the many myths and exaggerated claims surrounding AI.

## What I built

Claritas turns AI concepts into approachable, interactive experiences, including:

- AI basics: what AI is, the different types of AI, prompt engineering, and common limitations
- AI myths: exploring fears and misconceptions such as AI “thinking,” hallucinations, bias, job replacement, and overconfidence
- AI architecture: tokenization, context windows, neural networks, composition, and prediction
- Interactive prediction demos that show how models generate likely next tokens
- Scenario-based stories about using AI in everyday life, school, writing, and decision-making
- An AI vending machine simulation that demonstrates how confidently wrong AI behavior can become in the real world
- A provider Settings screen for choosing Apple Intelligence, OpenAI, or an OpenAI-compatible API

The app natively uses Apple Intelligence on supported devices. For people whose devices do not support Apple Intelligence—or for people who prefer an API-based experience—Claritas can seamlessly use the OpenAI API or another OpenAI-compatible API with a base URL, model ID, and API key.

## Who it helps

Claritas is designed for non-technical people, students, families, educators, and anyone who wants a clearer mental model of AI. The goal is to replace fear and hype with practical understanding: what AI can do, what it cannot do, and how to stay in control while using it.

## How it will be used

The app can be used for self-guided learning, classroom demonstrations, workshops, or conversations about responsible AI. I plan to release Claritas on the App Store shortly after final testing and App Store review.

## How Codex helped

Codex helped with most of the code and implementation. I made the architectural choices alongside Codex, including the SwiftUI structure, educational flow, Apple Intelligence integration, OpenAI-compatible API configuration, interactive demos, and simulator-ready Xcode project setup.
